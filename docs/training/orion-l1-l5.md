## Role in the Platform

L1-L5 represents role progression, not a direct score. Advancement requires a versioned framework, published requirements, a frozen evidence packet, learner-visible review, and authorized human approval.

B1-B5 competency bands may contribute evidence to progression requirements, but there is no automatic B-to-L conversion and no arithmetic average that creates an approved level.

## Canonical Rules

- Every progression review pins the target level and exact framework/requirement version.
- Only an authorized human review can approve a level change.
- AI suggestions, completion counts, averages, points, simulator scores, call volume, or sales value cannot independently approve advancement.
- Missing opportunity, unavailable evidence, technical failure, and disputed evidence must remain distinct from demonstrated deficiency.
- Decided reviews are historically traceable; reconsideration or appeal creates a linked review rather than erasing the original.
- Approved level history is effective-dated per learner employment episode and role scope.
- Rehire does not automatically transfer an old approved level into the new employment episode.
