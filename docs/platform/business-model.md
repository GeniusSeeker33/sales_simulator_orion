# Business Model

> Migrated from `GeniusSeeker33/geniusseeker-talent-success-platform/docs/business-model.md` during repository consolidation. Commercial concepts remain proposals until approved by Orion leadership, Finance, and legal counsel as applicable.

## Enterprise Licensing
Working concept: Genius Seeker licenses the platform to Orion on a per-seat basis. Preliminary pricing discussed is approximately $35 per seat. Final pricing should be validated against scope, support requirements, infrastructure cost, and enterprise value.

## Orion Rebate Concept
A possible commercial structure includes an approximately 7% rebate on qualifying platform invoices. This must be reviewed with finance and legal counsel before implementation.

## Training Services
Genius Seeker may provide curriculum development, live and virtual training, coaching, assessment, training analytics, and performance consulting.

## Talent Placement
Strong candidates who are not a fit for Orion may remain in the Genius Seeker talent network for placement with other employers. A possible model is an external placement fee with an agreed Orion revenue share for qualified referrals originating through the Orion relationship. A working concept discussed is approximately 7% of Genius Seeker's placement fee. This should be a separate commercial agreement and reviewed for conflicts of interest, ownership, and referral rules.

## Virtual Workforce
Longer-term, Genius Seeker may employ or contract a remote sales workforce supporting Orion under a services agreement. Compensation can combine hourly or block-based activity pay, successful-contact incentives, sales incentives, milestone bonuses, and progression-based compensation.
