# Fractional Team Governance Plan

> Migrated from `GeniusSeeker33/geniusseeker-talent-success-platform/contributors/fractional-team-governance-plan.md` during repository consolidation. Documentation only; no agreements, compensation formulas, worker-classification decisions, or appointments are activated by migration.

## 1. Executive Summary

Use approved projects and work items to organize fractional contributions, identified reviewers to confirm time and deliverables, and separate written agreements to govern compensation and IP. Contribution records provide evidence; they do not create payment or ownership rights.

## 2. Fractional Team Roles

Support trainer, virtual trainer, business analyst, product owner, AI workflow designer, software developer, QA / operational tester, reporting/data specialist, finance/controls advisor and subject-matter expert.

One verified person may hold several dated project role assignments. Each specifies scope, responsibilities, authority limits and applicable agreement references. A role describes work, not legal status, ownership or blanket system access. Proposed named roles remain unconfirmed until the participant and authorized sponsor agree.

## 3. Operating Records

| Record | Function |
|---|---|
| Contributor profile | Verified person, sponsor, participation state and restricted agreement/source references |
| Role assignment | Contributor's approved role, project, dates and responsibility limits |
| Project | Approved objective, scope, accountable owner, review authority and planning envelope |
| Work item | Bounded work, acceptance criteria, participants, planned hours, versioned deliverables, outcome and maintenance references |
| Time entry | Actual reported time against project/work/role, with separate approved hours and decision reference |
| Review | Authorized human decision against exact target/evidence revisions, with rationale and reconsideration trail |
| Milestone | Approved checkpoint supported by accepted work and any required verified outcome evidence |

Use immutable record IDs and verified `person_id`, organization/project scope and preserved source IDs/environment context. External participants do not require fabricated employment episodes. Agreements, private evidence and financial payloads stay in their authoritative restricted sources.

## 4. Contribution Workflow

**Approved project → approved work item → contribution/time → review → deliverable/milestone acceptance → optional compensation eligibility review**

Before substantive work, confirm participant identity, written terms, project role and approval authority. Contributor submissions preserve actual time and exact artifacts separately. Reviewers decide time and deliverable acceptance independently; milestones require their published criteria, not simply spent hours.

If an applicable agreement warrants compensation review, refer the accepted evidence to an authorized agreement/Finance reviewer. No automatic handoff or payout follows acceptance. Returned or disputed submissions retain their original reports and a correction route. Approval to perform work does not authorize production deployment.

## 5. Compensation Boundary

- Contribution evidence does not create payment rights; approved hours are not necessarily payable hours.
- A separate approved written agreement controls compensation and its method. Accepted deliverables do not automatically earn bonuses.
- Revenue participation requires its **own** executed agreement; a working draft or milestone completion supplies no entitlement.
- Eligibility review pins agreement/version, clause, evidence revisions and period/basis. It does not calculate or execute payment.
- Payment systems own instructions and actual paid status; Finance owns reconciliation, and accounting systems own entries. Business Central remains authoritative where applicable.
- Payroll/contractor payment administration remains separate from any future revenue-participation process.
- No equity, royalty, tax, worker-classification or legal-terms automation. No bank/payment credentials in contributor records.

## 6. IP & Ownership Guardrails

GitHub commits do not determine ownership. Reported or approved hours do not determine ownership. “Module ownership” means operational maintenance/accountability only. Written agreements govern IP; reference their applicable versions rather than inferring rights from activity, role, adoption or acceptance.

Unresolved terms go to the authorized agreement owner before substantial work. This plan neither assigns IP nor interprets legal ownership.

## 7. Approval & Audit Controls

- **No self-approval:** a lead who also contributes needs another qualified authorized reviewer.
- Pin exact work criteria, artifact/time revisions and agreement/evidence versions used for each decision.
- Preserve reported hours, approved hours and planned allowances separately. Pending approval is null, not zero.
- Keep deliverable acceptance distinct from observed business value. Unknown outcomes are not verified outcomes.
- Corrections and reconsideration create traceable revisions/decisions; never erase original submissions or silently substitute current agreement terms for past ones.
- Review rejection is an operational decision, not a determination that time need not be compensated.
- Check duplicate evidence/instructions and source status before any authorized external processing.
- Contributors see their permitted records and reasons; project peers see only necessary shared work. Restrict agreements, disputes and payment references; approve retention and access auditing.

## 8. Pilot Team Application

Start with a small approved Genius Seeker/Orion project and a few bounded work items: training-material preparation, requirements/data definitions and a separately authorized implementation/test task. Assign supported roles according to agreed availability; one person may cover several functions while independent review remains intact.

For example, a trainer submits session materials and time; a developer submits a versioned artifact and time; QA/SME reviewers assess relevant deliverables. Record follow-up or maintenance ownership and inspect outcomes later. Hours approved today do not prove future learner improvement or business benefit.

Later external contributors use the same person/profile, project scope, role assignments, review and agreement references, with isolated client access. They are not presumed employees or contractors, and scaling the team does not weaken independent approval or grant cross-client visibility.

## 9. Open Leadership / Legal / Finance Decisions

| Decision | Responsible owner |
|---|---|
| Sponsors, delegated authority, independent reviewers, review cadence and reconsideration route | Leadership / project owners |
| Applicable executed compensation/IP agreements and versions; participant status determined outside software | Authorized Legal / HR / agreement owners |
| Contributor registry/source aliases, project/client boundaries and access/retention | Leadership / IT / privacy |
| Planning allowances, time evidence, out-of-scope work and scope-change process | Project owners / Finance |
| Deliverable/outcome criteria, maintenance/handoff duties and sensitive artifact handling | Product / training / technical owners |
| Agreement, payee, payment and accounting authorities; authorized administrative handoff | Finance / Legal |
| Future participation eligibility evidence and revenue scope; separately reviewed terms/process | Legal / Finance / leadership |
| Duplicate/batched processing, amendments, post-payment corrections and reconciliation controls | Finance / payment operators |

These decisions remain open for adoption; this document neither resolves them nor authorizes implementation.
